The team

Four founders. One oven.

Slicers is the company we started in our Mundelein High School Incubator class: three seniors and a junior. Here’s who’s who.

  • Logan Holby

    Founder & CEO

    Logan started his first company, LYFTED, in 8th grade and now runs Nexus Pen, an AI smart pen that won Pitch Night 2026 with his Incubator team. At Slicers he leads the team, the product and the plan.

  • Christian Saucedo

    COO

    Christian runs operations: the systems, service standards and launch plan that make a new menu every week possible. He studies cybersecurity and IT and brings real customer-service experience from his job as a service advisor.
